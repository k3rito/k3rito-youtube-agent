# Mandatory Quality Gates

## Editorial

- Claims are traceable to sources.
- Contradictions and uncertainty are preserved.
- No fabricated source, quotation, statistic, or attribution.
- Channel tone, locale, audience, and topic policies pass.
- High-risk topics receive elevated review.

## Rights

- Every asset has provenance and compatible license evidence.
- Attribution requirements are satisfied.
- Voice/likeness use is authorized.
- Music, fonts, stock, and generated media restrictions are checked.

## Media

- Output file exists and is readable by FFprobe.
- Resolution/frame rate/duration meet job specification.
- Audio/video sync within configured tolerance.
- No unintended black/frozen frames.
- Loudness, clipping, silence, subtitle timing, and scene coverage pass.
- Thumbnail meets format/size/text safety requirements.
- Output hash and tool versions recorded.

## Publishing

- Channel ID and OAuth connection match.
- Budget/quota reservation exists.
- Metadata validated and no misleading claims.
- Privacy/schedule settings match channel policy.
- QC report passed.
- Publish result confirmed by YouTube before status is marked published.
