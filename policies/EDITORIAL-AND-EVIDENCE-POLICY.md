# Editorial, Evidence, and Fact-Checking Policy

Every material factual claim must have a claim record and one or more source references. Store source URL, title, publisher, author where available, publication date, retrieval time, relevant locator/excerpt, and evidence assessment.

Evidence labels:

- `PRIMARY`: original paper, official dataset, original document, direct statement.
- `AUTHORITATIVE_SECONDARY`: recognized institution or expert synthesis.
- `SECONDARY`: reputable reporting or review.
- `WEAK`: unsourced summaries, snippets, forums, social posts.
- `CONFLICTED`: credible sources disagree.
- `UNVERIFIED`: not adequately supported.

Search snippets are discovery aids, not final evidence for high-impact claims. Do not invent URLs, quotations, citations, statistics, study findings, or expert consensus. Preserve uncertainty and disagreement. Medical, legal, financial, safety, political, and breaking-news topics require elevated review and may require human review before publication.
