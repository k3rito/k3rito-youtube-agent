# Media Asset Rights Policy

Every asset must include provenance and usage metadata before it can enter a publishable timeline:
`asset_id`, `asset_type`, `origin`, `source_url_or_job_id`, `creator_or_provider`, `license_name`, `license_url_or_terms_snapshot`, `attribution_required`, `commercial_use_allowed`, `derivative_allowed`, `territory`, `expiry`, `channel_id`, `review_status`.

Unknown, expired, restricted, or incompatible licenses block publication. A generated asset is not automatically free of third-party rights or platform disclosure obligations. Music, fonts, stock footage, and voice likenesses require explicit rights review.
