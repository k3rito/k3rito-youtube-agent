# Comment Moderation Policy

Classify comments into normal, question, feedback, spam, harassment, threat, self-harm, personal-data exposure, and other high-risk categories. Apply rate limits and duplicate detection.

- Draft-only is the default.
- Automated replies require explicit channel-level enablement.
- Never reveal private information, provide high-stakes individualized advice, or claim to be a human channel owner.
- Threats, self-harm, credible danger, personal data, legal threats, and ambiguous high-risk content are escalated rather than auto-replied to.
- Deletion, hiding, reporting, or blocking actions require explicit policy authorization and audit logs.
